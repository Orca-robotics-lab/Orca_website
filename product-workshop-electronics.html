Workshop on Fundamentals of Electronics

Most people learn electronics from a textbook and never build circuits. In this live online workshop on Sunday, 29 November 2026, you will build circuits with your own hands and watch electricity come alive on an oscilloscope.

  • A starter components kit is shipped to your address, so you build along with us step by step.
  • Build an automatic night light, a slow dimming LED and an OR gate yourself
  • Watch live oscilloscope demonstrations of a capacitor discharge curve and a half wave rectifier with a filter
  • No prior electronics knowledge needed

What you will build yourself

With your shipped kit:

  • An automatic night light using an LDR, which switches on in the dark and off in the light
  • A slow dimming LED powered by a capacitor releasing its stored energy
  • An OR gate, to see how digital logic works
What we will demonstrate live

Watch on screen:

  • The capacitor's discharge curve plotted on an oscilloscope
  • A half wave rectifier with a filter, shown on the oscilloscope

The oscilloscope is not included in the shipped kit. These demonstrations are performed by the instructor while you watch, so you can see what is happening inside the circuits you build.

Along the way you will understand voltage, current, resistance, capacitance and semiconductors by building circuits, not by memorising concepts.

What is in your kit
  • Breadboard
  • 9V battery
  • 9V battery connector
  • LDR
  • LED
  • Resistor
  • 10K potentiometer
  • Capacitor
  • Diode
  • Pushbutton
  • Push type connector
  • Jumper wires

No oscilloscope or multimeter is included.

Who it is for

Students, beginners and curious builders. No prior electronics knowledge needed. All you need is a laptop or phone and an internet connection.
