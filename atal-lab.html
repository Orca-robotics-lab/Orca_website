03 OFFERINGS
A—01 Product

Electronics Learning Bundle

A comprehensive electronics learning system designed to help students understand electronics from the ground up through hands-on experimentation and guided learning.

— What's Included

  • 590-page electronics handbook covering fundamental concepts
  • 40+ hands-on activities and experiments
  • Complete electronics component kit for all activities
  • Digital oscilloscope for signal visualization
  • Modules covering diodes, rectifiers, capacitors, inductors & more
  • Structured learning pathway to build strong foundations

— Learning Outcomes

Students build circuits, perform experiments, and observe real electronic behaviour — visualizing charging curves, analyzing rectifier outputs, and developing intuitive understanding of how electronic systems work.

A—02 Product

Robotics Learning Bundle

A project-based robotics learning system where students build and program a complete robotic arm from scratch.

— What's Included

  • All components to build a 2-DOF robotic arm
  • Robotic arm structure and base assembly
  • Servo motors, ESP8266 controller & power supply
  • Wiring and connection components
  • Complete build guide and instruction manual
  • Programming resources and sample code

— Learning Outcomes

Students gain practical experience in robot assembly, electronics integration, programming, and motion control while building a functional robotic system from the ground up.
