About ORCA.

Orca Robotics is a robotics education company based in Karnataka, India, creating learning experiences, educational products, and workshops for future builders and innovators.

Vision

To create a generation of builders who understand robotics and engineering from the ground up, empowering them to innovate, solve problems, and create the technologies of tomorrow.

Mission

To create robotics learning experiences that build strong foundations, eliminate gaps in knowledge, and empower learners to understand, build, and innovate through hands-on exploration and real-world projects.

— Our Story

Built in Hubballi.

Founded in 2025, Orca Robotics began with a simple idea: the best way to learn robotics is by building, experimenting, and creating. Inspired by a passion for engineering and a desire to make technical learning more engaging, we started creating workshops, products, and resources that help learners explore the world of robotics through creation and discovery.

Today, we continue to work with schools, colleges, and aspiring builders to make robotics more accessible and engaging for everyone.

"We don't just teach about robots. We teach how to build them."
